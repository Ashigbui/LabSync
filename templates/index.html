About the space

A place to imagine, experiment, and build.

Opened in 2018, Ashesi’s Fab Lab provides a dedicated space where students can design and fabricate products, develop prototypes, and turn academic concepts into practical solutions.

The building brings together laboratories and makerspaces for Engineering and Computer Science students and faculty. It supports hands-on learning, interdisciplinary collaboration, creativity, and the development of ideas for projects and startups.
